# Operations workspace

Mode: Operate. Audience: internal export staff. Primary task: take Colombian coffee and cacao from product and lot records through a customer order, approval, and export documents.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Treat each export order as a live manifest: the queue shows what needs attention, and one selected row reveals its product, lot, client, approval, and document trail.

OWN-WORLD: An operations ledger built from a cool ink-blue workspace, bright paper surfaces, and a restrained citrus-orange action color. Use table rules, compact serial typography for codes, and verified state labels; no decorative terminal or glass motifs.

STORY: Operators scan the queue, open a request, verify the customer and reserved lot, then advance the order or produce its documents. Managers see pending approvals as a distinct action queue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A persistent left navigation rail; a top bar with search, workspace identity, and account; the main area begins with “Cola de exportación”, a short status summary and filters, followed by a two-pane queue and selected-order dossier with a vertical traceability rail.

FORM: Candidate 7, the grounded “Manifiesto operativo” structure, selected because it maps queue → dossier → next action directly to the 15 selected stories. Direction seed: a0609042. The assigned fixed-glyph treatment was rejected because it would make data-entry forms and responsive operational tables harder to read.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
